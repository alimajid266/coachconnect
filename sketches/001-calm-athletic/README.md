## Variant: Calm Athletic

### Design stance
An airy, editorial marketplace that makes trust and clear service information feel more important than urgency.

### Key choices
- Layout: spacious split hero, simple category tiles, three-column coach cards
- Typography: Georgia for warm editorial headings and system sans-serif for clarity
- Color: deep green, cream, muted mint, restrained gold
- Interaction: responsive menu, working sport filters, search feedback, coach-profile dialog

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: trust, readability, calm decision-making, premium but approachable tone
- Weak at: feels less energetic than a performance-focused sports brand

### Best for
Athletes and families who need confidence, clarity, and privacy before contacting a coach.
