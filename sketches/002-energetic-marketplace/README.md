## Variant: Energetic Marketplace

### Design stance
A bolder, action-led sports marketplace with compact cards and visibly interactive discovery controls.

### Key choices
- Layout: dramatic dark hero, raised search console, card-first coach marketplace, side-drawer profile
- Typography: heavy system sans-serif for fast scanning and sports energy
- Color: navy, coral and lime with pale blue supporting surfaces
- Interaction: responsive menu, full search console, working sport filters, profile drawer

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: momentum, prominent search, clear calls to action, portfolio/demo impact
- Weak at: more visually assertive and slightly denser than the calm option

### Best for
Athletes who want to act quickly and a brand that wants a modern sports-performance identity.
