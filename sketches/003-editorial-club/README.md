## Variant: Editorial Club

### Design stance
A calm, premium coaching club where trust and philosophy matter more than marketplace density.

### Key choices
- Layout: editorial split hero and three-column story cards
- Typography: Playfair Display with restrained sans-serif UI
- Color: warm parchment, forest green, terracotta
- Interaction: sport filters, profile/toast actions

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: trust, premium positioning, parent/family appeal
- Weak at: fast comparison of many coaches

### Best for
A curated, high-trust coaching service.
